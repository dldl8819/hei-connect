# Connect Platform Concept

Connect should feel like a familiar live community platform, but with
accessibility and safety as the default.

## Core Idea

Anyone can create a channel, open a live room, and choose the room purpose:

- Audio room: anyone can become a DJ and host voice-first rooms.
- Daily help: read signs, check surroundings, choose products, navigate a place.
- Conversation: casual voice/video rooms for people who want company.
- Broadcast: one-to-many live sharing with moderation, including screen sharing.
- Running guide: safety-critical room with stricter controls and stop cues.

Every live room also chooses a source mode:

- Audio-only: DJ rooms and voice communities.
- Screen share: desktop or browser broadcasting for computer screens.
- Selfie camera: front-facing camera for talking and community rooms.
- World camera: rear-facing camera for showing the host's surroundings.

## Product Shape

- Home: channels, live rooms, trusted contacts, scheduled sessions, and
  emergency access.
- Live room: video/audio, voice cues, optional chat, participant list.
- Sharing layer: short join codes, share links, and join-code entry for channels
  and live rooms.
- Trust layer: friends, verified volunteers, communities, and block/report.
- Creator layer: support-enabled channels and supporter messages.
- Safety layer: connection warnings, stop cue, moderation, consent, no recording
  by default.

## MVP Boundary

The first build should support private or invite-only channels and rooms. Public
discovery, large broadcasts, screen recording, real-money support, and automated
AI assistance should come after trust, reporting, payment policy, fraud handling,
and moderation basics exist.
