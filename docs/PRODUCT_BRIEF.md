# Product Brief

## Goal

Build Connect: an accessibility-first live platform where disabled and
non-disabled people can create channels, open live rooms, become audio DJs,
share camera or screen feeds, ask for help, guide each other, talk, broadcast,
and receive supporter contributions. The first safety-critical use case is
phone-based remote running guidance, and the long-term direction includes AI
smart glasses support.

## Primary Users

- Host: a person who opens a live room for help, DJ audio, conversation, or
  broadcast.
- Supporter: a person who joins a live room to assist, guide, or talk.
- Channel owner: a person or group that keeps a persistent live space.
- Creator: a host who regularly broadcasts and receives support.
- Moderator: a trusted operator for safety, abuse reports, and pilot programs.
- Runner and running guide: specialized roles inside running-guide rooms.

## MVP Promise

The app helps a host create a channel or quick live room, then share audio,
camera, or screen with trusted supporters. It also supports audio-only DJ rooms
and support-enabled creator channels. For running-guide rooms, it helps a runner
share a forward-facing phone camera feed with a guide and receive short voice
cues. It does not replace mobility skills, guide runners, guide dogs, or local
safety judgment.

## MVP Non-Goals

- Autonomous navigation.
- Road crossing decisions.
- Crowded marathon support without backup.
- Background recording.
- Unmoderated public help marketplace.
- AI-only guidance.
- Medical, emergency, or legal advice workflows.
- Real payment processing before policy, tax, payout, and fraud review.

## First Safe Test Context

- Outdoor track.
- Closed park loop without vehicles.
- Backup person nearby during early tests.
- Clear route, daylight, and stable network.
